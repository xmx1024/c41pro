FAQ

I don't hear agitation cues when the phone is locked.

Allow notifications for C41 Pro in Settings → Notifications. With the app open, cues are spoken aloud even when the ringer switch is off; turn the volume up.

Why is my developer time longer than the chart?

Each time developer is reused, the kit's instructions extend the time by a percentage that depends on the batch size. The app applies this automatically using the rolls logged to the batch.

How much chemistry should I pour?

Choose your tank under Develop → Settings. The plan shows the volume for each bath. Leave an air gap so inversions can move the chemistry.

Can I develop pushed and normal rolls in the same session?

Yes. Add each roll with its push or pull, and the app splits them into tank loads, each with its own time. Pushed rolls go first by default so they get the freshest developer.
