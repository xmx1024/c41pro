Never miss an inversion

"Invert ×4" prompts arrive right on schedule with vibration and a spoken cue, even with the ringer off. The pattern changes with your developer temperature, and you get a warning to start draining before each step ends.

Push and pull, one session

Mix pushed and normal rolls in one session. C41 Pro splits them into tank loads, develops pushed rolls first while the chemistry is freshest, and gives each tank its own exact time, from pull −1 to push +3.

Reuse, handled

Every roll is logged against its chemistry batch. Developer time is extended automatically as the batch is reused, and you're warned as it nears capacity or gets old.

Know what to pour

Pick your tank and C41 Pro tells you how much chemistry each bath needs. Presets for the Paterson Universal and eTone 2-reel tanks, adjustable for any tank.

From first pour to final rinse

  1. Log your chemistry when you mix a kit.
  2. Add your rolls: film stock, format and push/pull.
  3. Pour & start. Follow the cues through developer, blix, wash and stabilizer.

Which chemistry does C41 Pro support?

The CineStill CS41 kit only, liquid or powder. All times, agitation, reuse and capacity rules come from its instructions. Other C-41 kits use different times, so don't use C41 Pro with them yet. Support for more kits is planned.

I don't hear agitation cues when the phone is locked.

Allow notifications for C41 Pro in Settings → Notifications. With the app open, cues are spoken aloud even when the ringer switch is off; turn the volume up.

Why is my developer time longer than the chart?

Each time developer is reused, the kit's instructions extend the time by a percentage that depends on the batch size. The app applies this automatically using the rolls logged to the batch.

How much chemistry should I pour?

Choose your tank under Develop → Settings. The plan shows the volume for each bath. Leave an air gap so inversions can move the chemistry.

Can I develop pushed and normal rolls in the same session?

Yes. Add each roll with its push or pull, and the app splits them into tank loads, each with its own time. Pushed rolls go first by default so they get the freshest developer.

I ran a demo session. How do I stop it counting against my chemistry?

In History, swipe left on the run and tap Delete. The batch's usage and next developer time go back as if it never happened.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
