Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

C41 Pro does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your chemistry batches, development history, roll names and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders for agitation and step changes. These are created on your device and never leave it.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

The app has no sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. It works fully offline.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.
