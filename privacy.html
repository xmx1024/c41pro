Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

C41 Pro does not collect, sell or share any personal data. We never receive your data.

What the app stores

Your chemistry batches, development history, roll names, notes and settings are stored on your device.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, this data is also kept in your own private iCloud storage so it stays in sync between your devices. It is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and only you can access it; we cannot see or retrieve it. You can turn this off in the iOS Settings app under your name › iCloud. Deleting the app deletes the copy on that device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders for agitation and step changes. These are created on your device and never leave it. While a session runs, its countdown can also appear on the Lock Screen as a Live Activity, which is likewise local to your device.

Diagnostics

iOS may give the app anonymous reports about its launch times, hangs and crashes on your device. They stay on your device and are never sent to us automatically. You can choose to share them from the app's Settings, for example when asking for support.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

The app has no sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. It works fully offline; iCloud sync resumes when you're back online.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.
